Privacy Policy

Seri Helper — Supporting sericulture farmers with weather information and yield estimates.

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Seri Helper accesses location information, uses weather services, and handles weather data and estimated yield results.

Application: Seri Helper

Developer: Polymathic System Labs

Contact: polymathicsystemlabs@gmail.com

1. Introduction

Seri Helper is an application designed to help adult users, particularly sericulture farmers, estimate potential production yield using relevant weather information and other inputs used by the application.

This Privacy Policy describes how the application handles information when you use its features. By using the application, you acknowledge this policy. Where applicable, we will request permissions or consent as required by law and applicable platform policies.

2. Information We Access and Process

Seri Helper does not require users to create an account or log in to use its features.

The application may access or process the following:

  • Device location: The application uses the device's GPS location to determine the relevant local weather conditions. Location access is used for the application's weather-related functionality.
  • Weather information: Weather information, including temperature and humidity, is retrieved for use by the application.
  • Yield estimates: The application processes weather information and relevant calculation inputs to produce approximate yield estimates.
  • Backend information: Weather information and calculated yield results may be stored or processed using Supabase, the application's backend service.

We do not intentionally request your name, email address, or phone number for account registration because the application does not require user accounts.

3. Location Permission and Usage

Seri Helper accesses your device's location when required for its weather-related functionality. This allows the application to determine the relevant local environmental conditions for yield estimation.

Location access is subject to the permissions you grant through your device. You can manage or revoke location permission through your Android device settings. If you deny or disable location access, weather-dependent features may not work as intended.

The application is intended to use location for its stated functionality, not to track users continuously. This policy does not authorize background location collection beyond what the application actually implements.

The application is not intended to send raw GPS coordinates directly to an external weather provider. However, the location information used in a weather request may depend on the application's implementation. Any locality, coordinates, or other location-related query sent by the application or its backend to a third-party service must be disclosed and handled according to that service's applicable privacy terms.

4. WeatherAPI

Seri Helper uses WeatherAPI to obtain weather information relevant to its features.

Weather requests may involve location-related search information so that the appropriate weather conditions can be retrieved. Depending on the request method and backend configuration, the weather provider may process request information, including technical information associated with the connection.

WeatherAPI processes information according to its own service terms and privacy practices. Users should review the provider's privacy policy for details about its handling of request information.

WeatherAPI website: https://www.weatherapi.com/

We do not control the independent privacy practices of third-party service providers.

5. Supabase and Data Storage

Seri Helper uses Supabase as a backend service for processing and storing relevant application information. The information covered by this functionality includes weather information and calculated yield results, as configured in the application.

The information stored, its retention period, and the method used to associate records with application activity depend on the application's backend configuration.

Supabase may process technical information necessary to provide its infrastructure and services. Its handling of such information is subject to its own privacy terms.

Supabase website: https://supabase.com/

We do not intentionally use Supabase to maintain user registration accounts for Seri Helper because login is not required.

6. How We Use Information

Information processed by the application is used, as applicable, to:

  • Retrieve relevant weather conditions.
  • Support sericulture-related yield estimation.
  • Store or process weather information and calculated results.
  • Operate, maintain, and troubleshoot application functionality.
  • Protect the application and its backend services.

We do not intend to use device location for advertising or user tracking.

7. Sharing and Disclosure of Information

Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the application, including WeatherAPI for weather information and Supabase for backend functionality.

These providers may process information necessary to respond to requests or provide their services. The exact information transmitted depends on the application's implementation and backend configuration.

We do not sell users' personal information. We may disclose information where required by applicable law or where reasonably necessary to protect the security and integrity of the application, subject to applicable legal requirements.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Weather information and calculated yield results may be retained in the backend for as long as necessary for the application's intended functionality, subject to the configured storage and retention practices.

Because users do not need accounts, there is no in-app account deletion process. If you have a question about information associated with your use of Seri Helper, or wish to request deletion of data that can reasonably be identified and deleted, contact us at polymathicsystemlabs@gmail.com .

We will review and handle such requests in accordance with applicable law, technical capabilities, and any legitimate retention obligations. Data held independently by third-party service providers may also be subject to their respective retention policies.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable steps, appropriate to the application, to protect information handled by our systems. Security depends on the application's implementation, backend access controls, service configuration, and other technical measures.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of information processed by the application or independent third-party providers.

10. Children's Privacy

Seri Helper is intended for adults and is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly intend to collect personal information from children through the application.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the application, contact us so that we can review the matter and take appropriate action where required.

11. Your Choices and Rights

You can manage the application's location permission through your Android device settings. You may also stop using the application at any time.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of certain information. To submit a privacy-related request, contact us using the details provided below.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application, its data practices, or applicable legal requirements change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.

You should review this page periodically to remain informed about how information is handled.
